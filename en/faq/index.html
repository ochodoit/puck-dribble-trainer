Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what people ask before they start. Whether it is free, whether you need to install anything and whether the camera video is saved are at the top. For setup, see the guide with pictures.

What is Puck Dribble Trainer?

Puck Dribble Trainer is a free web game that uses the camera on your phone, iPad or laptop to track your puck and stick, so you can train ice hockey stickhandling at home. There is no app to install: it runs in the browser.

Is it free?

Yes, anyone can play for free. Only leaderboards and community posts are for members (who join with an invite code).

Do I need to install an app?

No. Open puck-trainer.com in Safari or Chrome and play. Press Add a shortcut on the first screen to put an icon on your desktop or home screen: it opens like an app, and once opened it works offline too (logging in and leaderboards need the internet).

Is the camera video saved or sent anywhere?

No. The video is only used on your device to find the puck. It is never saved or uploaded; only scores, records and settings are kept.

Which devices does it work on?

Any phone (iPhone or Android), iPad, tablet or laptop with a camera. Put it on a stand so it can see the floor.

Can I try it without a camera?

Yes. Press Try it without a camera on the first screen and your mouse or finger becomes the puck. Trial scores are not saved or ranked. If you like it, play with your camera and a real puck.

Do I need a real puck?

No. Hold a ball, a bottle cap or anything that stands out from the floor in the center circle for 3 seconds, and it becomes your puck.

What floor works best?

A white shooting pad or a plain, light mat works best. Other floors work too, as long as the puck color clearly differs from the floor.

Where and how high should the camera be?

Put it 1–1.5 m high (at least 80 cm), looking down at the floor at an angle. Lying on the floor it is too low, and the puck gets lost on the far side. If you move the camera, fit the area again.

Can I play on a big screen like a TV or a laptop?

Yes. The best setup is a laptop + USB webcam + HDMI to a TV. A phone or iPad can use an HDMI adapter (low delay) or wireless mirroring (may lag 0.1–0.3 s). See Big screens for every option, best first.

Does it work on vinyl, garage or rooftop concrete instead of a white floor?

Yes, no white floor is needed. But the puck color must also differ from stains, joints and shadows. Over 24 test positions an orange ball scored 24/24 on every floor; a black puck scored 24/24 on vinyl, 9/24 on garage concrete and 0/24 on a sunny rooftop. On concrete or outdoors, use an orange or neon ball.

Do I need lines on the floor?

No. Lines only help you fit the area the first time, and the area is kept for next time. Tape or coins on the four corners make fitting easy.

Can I play with just a phone or a tablet?

Yes, but the screen is hard to watch while playing, because tilting the camera toward the floor tilts the screen too. Use it for setup and light practice, and connect a TV to really play.

Can I put the camera beside the TV so it does not block the screen?

Yes. Once the four corners are fitted, a side view is almost as accurate as straight on (30° to the side at 1.2 m: about 3.6 cm off). Stay within 45°, and put the area’s two “front” corners on the floor edge nearest the TV.

Which puck or ball is tracked best?

A single-color puck or ball that clearly differs from the floor: black, orange or red on a white floor, white or neon on a dark mat. Multi-colored balls such as rainbow balls are not tracked well yet.

What happens when the stick or a skate covers the puck?

For a moment the puck stays on screen, faded, for up to 1.5 seconds, and nothing is scored. Register the stick color in Calibrate and a puck hidden next to the blade is followed too.

What age is it for?

There is no age limit. Leaderboards are split by age group from U8 to U18, adult and senior; young players should start with a parent.

Can I use it for inline or roller hockey?

Yes. It trains stickhandling with a puck or a ball, so it works for inline and roller hockey too.

How do I get on the leaderboard?

Finish a game (except Free Practice) while logged in as a member, and your best score goes on the world, country, team and age-group leaderboards. Guest scores are not ranked.

How do I get an invite code?

Get an invite link from a member friend (each member can invite 3 people). No link? Finish one game as a guest: the result screen shows how to join. You never need to post a video on social media.

How is it different from other camera stickhandling apps?

It runs in the browser with nothing to install, it is available in Korean and English, and all 7 games are free. See Compare apps for how it stacks up against Stick Control, Hockey Home, Hotdangle and others.

Is it available in English?

Yes. Press English on the first screen, or open puck-trainer.com/en/.

How is my personal data handled?

We only ask for a nickname, country, team, age and stick side, and never an email address. Visit counts (Google Analytics) never include personal details such as nicknames or ages.
