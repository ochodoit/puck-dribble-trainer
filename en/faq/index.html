Frequently asked questions

Puck Dribble Trainer is a free web game that uses the camera on your phone, iPad or laptop to track your puck and stick, so you can train ice hockey stickhandling at home. There is no app to install: it runs in the browser. The questions new players ask most about Puck Dribble Trainer.

What is Puck Dribble Trainer?

Puck Dribble Trainer is a free web game that uses the camera on your phone, iPad or laptop to track your puck and stick, so you can train ice hockey stickhandling at home. There is no app to install: it runs in the browser.

Is it free?

Yes. Anyone can play for free. Leaderboards and posting in the community are for members who joined with an invite code.

Do I need to install an app?

No. Open puck-trainer.com in a browser such as Safari or Chrome and play. Add it to your home screen and it opens full screen like an app.

Which devices does it work on?

Any iPad or tablet, iPhone or Android phone, or laptop with a camera. Put the device on a stand so it can see the floor.

Is the camera video saved or sent anywhere?

No. The video is used on your device only, to find the puck and the stick. It is never saved or uploaded. Only things like scores, records and settings are stored.

Do I need a real puck?

No. Hold a ball, a bottle cap or anything that stands out from the floor in the center circle for 3 seconds, and it learns that object as your puck.

What floor works best?

A white shooting pad or a plain, light-colored mat is tracked best. Other floors work once you fit the four corners of the play area. On any floor, the puck color must clearly differ from the floor for accurate tracking.

Where and how high should the camera be?

Put it 1–1.5 m high, looking down at the floor at an angle, so the floor area fills at least a third of the screen height. At least 80 cm is best. Too low, such as a device lying on the floor (50 cm or lower), squashes the far side of the floor flat: the puck becomes tiny and gets lost or is placed far off on the far side. If you move the camera, fit the area again.

Can I play on a big screen like a TV or a laptop?

Yes. The best setup is a USB webcam on a laptop: stand the webcam on its own at 1–1.5 m high and connect the laptop to the TV with HDMI. A phone or iPad can connect by cable with an HDMI adapter (low delay) or mirror wirelessly with AirPlay, Smart View or Chromecast (may lag 0.1–0.3 s). The guide lists every option, best first, under “Devices and big screens”.

Does it work on vinyl, garage or rooftop concrete instead of a white floor?

Yes. The app needs no white floor; it learns the floor color around the puck when you register it. But the puck color must differ not only from the floor itself but also from stains, joints and shadows on it. Tested over 24 positions: on vinyl both a black puck and an orange ball were tracked at all 24, but on garage concrete with oil stains and joints the black puck managed 9, and on a sunny rooftop with your shadow next to the puck it managed 0. The orange ball was tracked at all 24 on every floor. On concrete or outdoors use a strongly colored ball or puck such as orange or neon; on rough floors a street hockey ball or a roller puck rolls better.

Do I need lines on the floor?

No. Lines only help you place the four corners of the area; once fitted, the game does not need them, and the area is kept for next time. Putting tape or coins on the four corners makes fitting easy, and if they differ from the puck color you can leave them as boundary marks. If you move the camera, fit the area again.

Can I play with just a phone or a tablet?

You can, but watching the screen while playing is awkward. Tilting the device so the camera sees the floor tilts the screen down too, so it looks small and slanted when you stand. It is fine for setting up and light practice; to really enjoy it, connect a TV with an HDMI cable or wireless mirroring. Then you can also use the sharper rear camera.

Can I put the camera beside the TV so it does not block the screen?

Yes. Once the four corners of the area are fitted to the floor, an angled side view is corrected for perspective and accuracy is almost the same as straight on (1 m to the side, about 30°, 1.2 m high: about 3.6 cm). The best spot is on top of the TV, centered; beside the TV, keep it close and no more than 45° to the side. Put the two “front” corners of the area on the floor edge nearest the TV. After the 3/3 test move, the app tells you whether to raise the camera, that this is the lowest height, or that you may lower it a little.

Which puck or ball is tracked best?

A single-color puck or ball that clearly differs from the floor (black, orange or red on a white floor; white or neon on a dark mat). A two-tone puck such as a white center with a black rim also works, by the color that differs from the floor. Multi-colored balls such as rainbow balls are not tracked well yet. A color that also differs from your skates and shoes is even better.

What happens when the stick or a skate covers the puck?

If the puck is hidden for a moment inside the area, it stays on screen, faded, for up to 1.5 seconds, and no hits or points are counted meanwhile. Register the stick color in Calibrate too and a puck hidden next to the blade follows the blade. A black puck touching a black skate is cut out of the blob and tracked as well.

What age is it for?

There is no age limit. Leaderboards are split by age group from U8 to U18, adult and senior. Young players should start with a parent.

Can I use it for inline or roller hockey?

Yes. It trains stickhandling with a puck or a ball, so it works for inline and roller hockey practice too.

How do I get on the leaderboard?

Finish a game (except Free Practice) while logged in as a member and your best score goes on the world, country, team and age-group leaderboards. Guest scores are not ranked.

How do I get an invite code?

Get an invite link from a member friend (each member can invite up to 3 people). Or finish a game as a guest and the results screen shows how: post your play on social media and DM the link to our official Instagram, and we will send you a code.

Is it available in English?

Yes. Switch with the language buttons (Korean | English) on the first screen, or open the English address, puck-trainer.com/en/.

How is my personal data handled?

When you join we ask for a nickname, country, team, age and stick side. We use Google Analytics to count visits and plays, but no personal details such as nicknames or ages are sent.
