The 7 stickhandling games

Not sure where to start? See “Pick by goal” just below. In every game the camera follows your real puck (or ball), and each game trains a different skill.

Pick by goal

  • New to itArcade Easy · start with 1 minute
  • Faster handsQuick Hands · Wide Dribble
  • Turns and protecting the puckFigure 8 · Reaction Target
  • Head up, seeing the playDodge then try Invisible mode
  • A coach’s drill, exactlyFree Practice

What each game trains

01

Arcade

Dodge the falling red pucks side to side and collect coins. Every change of direction (a touch) scores. 1, 3 or 5 minutes.

Skills Side-to-side stickhandling · seeing the play while handling the puck · quick decisions

02

Dodge

Avoid red pucks flying in from all four sides. More come as time goes on.

Skills 360° puck control · stops and changes of direction · reading danger

03

Wide Dribble

Three drills in a row that take the puck all the way to each side. A touch counts when the puck enters a red zone drawn on your floor.

Skills Wide stickhandling · reach · rhythm

04

Figure 8

Take the puck through the gates in a figure 8 around two cones. You pick the first cone and the direction.

Skills Forehand–backhand transitions · protecting the puck · keeping control through turns

05

Quick Hands

Touch the puck side to side as fast as you can in a box at 3, 6 and 9 o’clock in turn, for the time you pick (1, 3 or 5 minutes) at each.

Skills Wrist speed · soft hands · short touches

06

Reaction Target

Get the puck to targets that pop up anywhere, as fast as you can. Reaction time and travel time are timed separately.

Skills Reaction speed · first touch · changing direction

07

Free Practice

Build your own course with numbered points and time every lap. Share it in the community and race other players.

Skills Any drill a coach wants · timed repetitions

Scores and leaderboards

Arcade, Dodge, Wide Dribble and Quick Hands let you pick 1, 3 or 5 minutes (Wide Dribble and Quick Hands run three rounds of that time). When a member finishes a game (except Free Practice), their best score goes on the world, country, team and age-group leaderboards; attendance and recent scores are in My info.
