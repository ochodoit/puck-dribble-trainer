The 7 stickhandling games

Puck Dribble Trainer is a free web game that uses the camera on your phone, iPad or laptop to track your puck and stick, so you can train ice hockey stickhandling at home. There is no app to install: it runs in the browser. In every game the camera follows your real puck (or ball). Wide Dribble and Quick Hands draw their zones on the real floor in the camera view, so your puck moves exactly like the puck on video. The other games show the whole area as a flat, top-down field, where pushing the puck toward the camera moves it up the screen.

What each game trains

01

Arcade

Dodge the red pucks falling down the lane and collect coins. Every change of direction (a touch) scores, and you compete on your score over 1, 3 or 5 minutes.

Skills Side-to-side stickhandling · seeing the play while handling the puck · quick decisions

02

Dodge

Avoid the red pucks flying in from all four sides of the field. More of them come at once as time goes on.

Skills 360° puck control · stops and changes of direction · reading danger

03

Wide Dribble

Three drills in a row that take the puck all the way to each side (edge to edge · short touches at both ends · both ends and the middle). The red zones are drawn on the real floor in the camera view, and a touch counts when the center of the puck enters the zone. Each drill starts when you hold the puck in the center for 3 seconds.

Skills Wide stickhandling · reach · rhythm

04

Figure 8

Skate the puck through the gates in a figure 8 around two cones. Pick which cone comes first (left or right) and clockwise or counterclockwise on the game screen.

Skills Forehand–backhand transitions · protecting the puck · keeping control through turns

05

Quick Hands

Touch the puck side to side as fast as you can inside the box drawn on the middle of your floor for 20 seconds. Touches outside the box do not count.

Skills Wrist speed · soft hands · short touches

06

Reaction Target

Get the puck to targets that pop up at random spots as fast as you can. Reaction time and travel time are recorded separately.

Skills Reaction speed · first touch · changing direction

07

Free Practice

Place numbered points to build your own course and time every lap (cycle). Share a course in the community and race other players on it.

Skills Any drill a coach wants · timed repetitions

Scores and leaderboards

Arcade, Dodge and Wide Dribble let you pick 1, 3 or 5 minutes. Members who joined with an invite code get their best score on the world, country, team and age-group leaderboards when they finish a game (except Free Practice), and can see their attendance and recent scores in My info.
