          First time? — How to play
          
    
            
  1. Set up — Put your phone, iPad or laptop on a stand 1–1.5 m high, looking down at the floor at an angle. Too low (50 cm or less) and the puck gets lost on the far side.
    2. 
            
  2. Area — Drag the four corners onto the floor. Next time the saved area is used as is.
    3. 
            
  3. Register puck — A ball, lid or puck: hold it in the center circle for 3 s. The more its color differs from the floor, the more accurate the tracking.
    4. 
            
  4. Test move — Take the puck to the four corner targets to check it is tracked everywhere, then pick a game.
    5. 
            
  5. Start — Hold the registered puck in the circle for 3 s to start hands-free. You can also mirror your phone to a TV.
    6. 
          

        

        
Puck Dribble Trainer is a free web game that uses the camera on your phone, iPad or laptop to track your puck and stick, so you can train ice hockey stickhandling at home. There is no app to install: it runs in the browser.

          Detection fine-tuning (only if the puck is not tracked well)
          

            
When you register the puck or stick in Calibrate, the tolerances are set automatically. In the preview, red is what is detected as the puck and green as the stick. If only the puck turns red, leave these alone.
